# SessionRecap

## 0.3.0

- Money shows with gold, silver and copper coins, as the game does: money in green, money out red, and figures still at zero dimmed.
- A level bar: experience in purple over rested in blue, with how far through the level you are and how long the rest will take at this session's pace.
- Most looted shows each item's icon, with its name in its quality colour.
- The window fits what it holds, growing as earlier sessions fill in, instead of leaving empty space.

## 0.2.0

- A minimap button opens the window. Drag it around the minimap; `/session button` hides it.
- Every login now says one line in chat: that counting has started, that the session is carrying on, or a summary of the last one.
- `/session debug` lists the money and loot events the game has sent this login, to explain a figure that stays at zero.
